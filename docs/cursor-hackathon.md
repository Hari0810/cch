​🧠 The Context
​Cybersecurity is entering an AI-native era.

​Security teams are leaning more and more on autonomous systems to detect threats, investigate incidents, analyse infrastructure, and automate repetitive work. At the same time, AI is opening up new attack surfaces and new defensive options.

​This hackathon digs into the future of AI-powered cybersecurity:

​How can autonomous agents improve security operations?

​Where should humans stay in the loop?

​What does trustworthy, secure AI infrastructure actually look like?

​How do we build safer systems for the next generation of software?

​Whether you're into critical infrastructure, digital forensics, cryptography, or incident response, this is your shot at prototyping the future of cybersecurity.
